What is the standard passport photo size?

Passport photograph dimensions depend on the country and document type. Some common digital formats include 35 × 45 mm and 2 × 2 inches. Always check the official authority's requirements before submitting a photograph.

Does this tool remove the photograph background?

No. This tool crops and resizes your photograph. Its white background option fills transparent canvas areas but does not automatically remove or replace an existing photograph background.

Are my photographs uploaded?

No. Your photograph is processed locally inside your browser. It is not sent to our website server.

What does 300 DPI mean?

DPI describes print resolution. This tool calculates pixel dimensions based on the selected print size and DPI. The downloaded image is sized in pixels; the DPI metadata itself is not embedded in the JPEG or PNG file.

Important photo requirements

Different passport and visa authorities may specify face size, head position, background colour, expression, lighting and image quality. This tool does not automatically verify official compliance or identify facial positioning.
