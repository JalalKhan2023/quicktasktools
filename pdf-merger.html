Use this free online PDF merger to combine multiple PDF documents into one file. Upload your PDF files, arrange them in your preferred order and merge them into a single document. The tool works directly in your browser without requiring software installation.

How to Merge PDF Files Online

  1. Select two or more PDF files from your device.
  2. Review the uploaded files and their sizes.

  4. Remove any files you do not want to include.
  5. Click Merge PDF Documents.
  6. Download your combined PDF document.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I combine multiple PDF files into one?

Upload at least two PDF files, arrange them in your preferred order and click Merge PDF Documents. The tool combines them into one downloadable PDF.

Can I change the order of PDF files before merging?

Yes. Use the Move Up and Move Down buttons to arrange your PDF documents before merging. The selected order determines the order in the final document.

Will merging PDFs preserve their original pages?

Yes. The merger copies the pages from each selected PDF into the combined document, preserving their page content.

Can I remove a PDF file before merging?

Yes. Click the Remove button beside any selected PDF file to exclude it from the final document.

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

No. The merging process takes place in your browser. Your selected PDF files are processed locally and are not uploaded to our server by this tool.

Can I merge password-protected PDF files?

This tool does not provide a password-entry feature. Password-protected or encrypted PDF files may fail to merge. Use an authorized, unlocked copy if you have permission to do so.
