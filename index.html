How to compress an image online

Our free image compressor helps reduce the file size of your photographs for online forms, applications, websites and email attachments.

  1. Select an image from your device.
  2. Enter your desired maximum file size in KB.
  3. Click Compress Image.
  4. Download your compressed JPG image.

Is my image private?

Yes. Image processing takes place locally in your browser using the Canvas API. Your selected image is not sent to our server.

Note: The tool exports images as JPEG. Transparency in PNG images will be replaced with a white background. Exact file-size matching is not always possible for every image; results may be slightly above the target in difficult cases.
