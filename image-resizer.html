Resize images to a specific KB size online

Many online applications, examination forms, job portals and government websites require photographs and documents within a specified file-size limit. This free image resizer helps you prepare images for those requirements.

How to resize an image to 50KB or 100KB

  1. Upload your JPG, PNG or WebP image.
  2. Select your required size, such as 50KB or 100KB.
  3. Adjust the image dimensions if necessary.
  4. Click Resize & Compress Image.
  5. Download your processed image.

Does this tool upload my image?

No. Image processing takes place locally in your browser using JavaScript and the Canvas API. Your selected image is not sent to our server.

Can every image be reduced to 20KB?

Not always at its original dimensions. Some detailed or high-resolution photographs require smaller dimensions to meet very low file-size limits. Our tool automatically reduces dimensions when necessary to meet your selected maximum size.

Is the output exactly the selected KB size?

The tool targets a maximum file size. For example, a 100KB target means the output will be no larger than 100KB. The resulting file may be smaller because image compression works in discrete steps.
